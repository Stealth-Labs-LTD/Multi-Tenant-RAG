# Q4 2025 Executive Business Summary

## Financial Highlights

### Revenue Performance
- **Total Revenue**: £12.4M (Q4 2025), up 18% year-over-year
- **Recurring Revenue (ARR)**: £42.1M, representing 85% of total revenue
- **Gross Margin**: 72%, up from 68% in Q4 2024
- **Net Revenue Retention**: 118%, indicating strong upsell and cross-sell performance

### Cost Structure
- **Operating Expenses**: £9.8M, with R&D at 32% of revenue
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: £2,400 per enterprise customer, down 15% from prior quarter
- **CAC Payback Period**: 11 months (target: under 14 months)
- **EBITDA**: £1.9M positive, third consecutive profitable quarter
