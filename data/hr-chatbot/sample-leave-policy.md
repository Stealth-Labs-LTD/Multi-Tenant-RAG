# Employee Leave Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Policy Number:** HR-POL-003
**Department:** Human Resources
**Approved By:** Chief People Officer

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the types of leave available to employees, eligibility criteria, accrual rules, and procedures for requesting and approving leave. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees unless stated otherwise.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers all employees of the organization across all departments and locations. Contractors and temporary staff should refer to the terms of their engagement for leave entitlements.

---

## 3. Annual Leave (Vacation)

### 3.1 Entitlement

| Tenure               | Annual Entitlement |
|-----------------------|--------------------|
| 0 - 2 years          | 20 working days    |
| 3 - 5 years          | 23 working days    |
| 6 - 10 years         | 25 working days    |
| 10+ years            | 28 working days    |

Part-time employees receive annual leave on a pro-rata basis according to their contracted hours.

### 3.2 Accrual

Annual leave accrues on a monthly basis. New employees begin accruing leave from their first day of employment. Accrued leave is available for use after the employee has completed their probationary period (typically 3 months).

### 3.3 Carry-Over

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year. Carry-over days must be used by March 31 of the new year. Any leave not used by this date will be forfeited unless an exception is approved by the employee's department head.

### 3.4 Requesting Annual Leave

- Submit leave requests through the HR portal at least 10 working days before the intended start date.
- Requests for more than 10 consecutive working days must be submitted at least 30 days in advance.
- Approval is subject to business needs and team coverage requirements.
- Managers must respond to leave requests within 5 working days.

### 3.5 Leave During Notice Period

Employees serving their notice period may only take annual leave during this time with explicit written approval from their manager and HR. Unused leave will be paid out in the final paycheck in accordance with local regulations.

## 4. Sick Leave

### 4.1 Entitlement

All full-time employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Sick leave does not accrue and unused sick days do not carry over to the following year.

### 4.2 Notification

- Employees must notify their manager as soon as practicable on the first day of absence, ideally before the start of their scheduled shift.
- Notification should include an estimated return date.
- For absences of 3 or more consecutive working days, a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required.

### 4.3 Extended Illness

For illnesses extending beyond the 10-day paid sick leave entitlement:

- Employees may apply for Short-Term Disability Leave (see Section 8).
- Unused annual leave may be used to cover additional sick days with manager approval.
- Unpaid sick leave may be granted at the discretion of the department head and HR.

### 4.4 Return to Work

After an absence of 5 or more consecutive working days due to illness, a return-to-work discussion with the employee's manager is required. A fitness-for-duty certificate may be required depending on the nature of the illness.

---

## 5. Parental Leave

### 5.1 Maternity Leave

- **Entitlement:** 16 weeks of paid maternity leave at full salary, plus an additional 10 weeks of unpaid leave if requested.
- **Eligibility:** Employees who have completed at least 12 months of continuous service.
- **Notification:** Written notice to HR at least 8 weeks before the expected due date.
- **Return Guarantee:** The employee is guaranteed the same or an equivalent position upon return.

### 5.2 Paternity/Partner Leave

- **Entitlement:** 4 weeks of paid leave at full salary within the first 12 months following the birth or adoption of a child.
- **Eligibility:** Employees who have completed at least 6 months of continuous service.
- **Notification:** Written notice to HR at least 4 weeks before the intended start date.

### 5.3 Adoption Leave

- **Entitlement:** Equivalent to maternity leave provisions (16 weeks paid, 10 weeks unpaid).
- **Eligibility:** Same as maternity leave.
- **Notification:** Written notice to HR within 2 weeks of the adoption placement date being confirmed.

### 5.4 Parental Leave (General)

In addition to maternity, paternity, and adoption leave, each parent is entitled to 2 weeks of unpaid parental leave per calendar year until the child reaches the age of 8. This leave can be taken in single-day increments.

---

## 6. Personal Leave and Compassionate Leave

### 6.1 Personal Leave

Employees are entitled to 3 paid personal days per calendar year for matters such as:

- Moving house
- Attending to urgent personal business
- Religious or cultural observances not covered by public holidays

### 6.2 Compassionate Leave (Bereavement)

- **Immediate family** (spouse, partner, parent, child, sibling): Up to 5 paid working days.
- **Extended family** (grandparent, grandchild, in-law, aunt, uncle): Up to 3 paid working days.
- Additional unpaid leave may be granted at the discretion of the employee's manager.

---

## 7. Public Holidays

The organization observes all gazetted public holidays in the jurisdiction where the employee is based. When a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday will typically be observed as the replacement holiday unless otherwise communicated.

Employees required to work on a public holiday will receive either time-in-lieu or penalty rates as defined by their employment agreement.

---

## 8. Long Service Leave

Employees who have completed 10 years of continuous service are entitled to 8 weeks of paid long service leave. An additional 4 weeks is granted for every subsequent 5 years of service. Long service leave must be taken in a minimum block of 2 consecutive weeks.

---

## 9. Short-Term Disability Leave

Employees who have exhausted their sick leave and require additional time off due to a medical condition may apply for Short-Term Disability Leave. This provides up to 12 weeks of leave at 75% of the employee's base salary, subject to medical certification and approval from HR.

---

## 10. Study Leave

Employees pursuing pre-approved education or professional development may be granted:

- Up to 5 paid study days per calendar year for exam preparation.
- Paid time off for scheduled exams.
- Approval must be obtained from both the employee's manager and HR prior to enrollment.

---

## 11. Jury Duty and Civic Obligations

Employees summoned for jury duty or required to attend court as a witness will receive full pay for the duration of their civic obligation. Proof of the summons must be provided to HR.

---

## 12. Domestic Violence Leave

Employees experiencing domestic or family violence are entitled to 10 days of paid leave per calendar year. This leave is available immediately upon employment and does not require a waiting period. All requests are handled with strict confidentiality.

---

## 13. Leave Without Pay

Where no paid leave balance is available, employees may request unpaid leave. Approval is at the discretion of the department head and HR. During unpaid leave exceeding 30 days, benefits coverage may be affected. Employees should consult HR for details.

---

## 14. General Provisions

### 14.1 Fraudulent Claims

Misuse of sick leave or fraudulent leave claims will be treated as serious misconduct and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

### 14.2 Record Keeping

All leave records are maintained in the HR management system. Employees can view their leave balances at any time through the self-service portal.

### 14.3 Amendments

This policy is reviewed annually. Amendments will be communicated to all employees via email and the company intranet at least 30 days before taking effect.
