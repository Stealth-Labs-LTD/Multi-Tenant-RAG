# Code of Conduct

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Policy Number:** HR-POL-001
**Applies To:** All employees, contractors, and third-party representatives

---

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct sets out the standards of behavior expected of everyone who works for or represents the organization. It reflects our commitment to integrity, respect, and accountability in all professional interactions.

## 2. Core Values

Our organization is guided by the following core values:

- **Integrity** -- We act honestly and ethically in all our dealings.
- **Respect** -- We treat everyone with dignity and courtesy.
- **Accountability** -- We take responsibility for our actions and decisions.
- **Collaboration** -- We work together to achieve shared goals.
- **Innovation** -- We encourage creative thinking and continuous improvement.

## 3. Professional Conduct

### 3.1 Workplace Behavior

All employees are expected to:

- Maintain a professional and courteous demeanor in all workplace interactions.
- Communicate openly, honestly, and constructively.
- Respect the time, space, and contributions of colleagues.
- Follow all organizational policies, procedures, and directives.
- Report concerns or potential violations through appropriate channels.

### 3.2 Attendance and Punctuality

Employees are expected to be reliable in attendance and punctual for work and meetings. Habitual tardiness or unexplained absences may result in disciplinary action.

## 4. Anti-Discrimination and Harassment

### 4.1 Equal Opportunity

The organization is committed to providing equal opportunity in employment. Discrimination based on race, color, religion, gender, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, disability, age, or any other protected characteristic is strictly prohibited.

### 4.2 Harassment

All forms of harassment, including but not limited to verbal, physical, visual, and sexual harassment, are prohibited. This includes conduct that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment.

### 4.3 Reporting

Employees who experience or witness discrimination or harassment should report it immediately to their manager, HR, or through the confidential ethics hotline. All reports will be investigated promptly and impartially. Retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith report is strictly prohibited.

## 5. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict, or could appear to conflict, with the interests of the organization. This includes:

- Outside employment that interferes with job responsibilities.
- Financial interests in competitors, suppliers, or customers.
- Personal relationships that could influence business decisions.

Any potential conflict of interest must be disclosed to the employee's manager and HR in writing.

## 6. Confidentiality and Data Protection

### 6.1 Confidential Information

Employees must protect confidential business information, including trade secrets, financial data, customer information, and strategic plans. Confidential information must not be disclosed to unauthorized parties during or after employment.

### 6.2 Data Privacy

All employees must comply with applicable data protection laws and organizational data privacy policies. Personal data must be collected, processed, and stored in accordance with our Privacy Policy and relevant regulations.

## 7. Use of Company Resources

Company resources, including equipment, software, email, and internet access, are provided for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with job duties, violate any policy, or compromise security.

## 8. Health and Safety

All employees share responsibility for maintaining a safe and healthy workplace. Employees must:

- Follow all health and safety policies and procedures.
- Report hazards, incidents, and near-misses immediately.
- Not attend work while under the influence of drugs or alcohol.
- Participate in required safety training.

## 9. Environmental Responsibility

The organization is committed to minimizing its environmental impact. Employees are encouraged to reduce waste, conserve energy, and support sustainability initiatives.

## 10. Compliance with Laws

All employees must comply with applicable laws and regulations in every jurisdiction where the organization operates. Ignorance of the law is not an excuse for non-compliance.

## 11. Reporting Violations

Employees who become aware of violations of this Code of Conduct, company policies, or applicable laws have a duty to report them. Reports can be made to:

- Direct manager or department head
- Human Resources
- Confidential ethics hotline: 1800-ETHICS-01
- Email: ethics@company.com

All reports are treated confidentially to the extent possible. The organization prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## 12. Consequences of Violations

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. The severity of the action will depend on the nature and circumstances of the violation.

## 13. Acknowledgment

All employees are required to read and acknowledge this Code of Conduct upon joining the organization and annually thereafter. Acknowledgment is recorded in the HR management system.
